      How the game plays
      • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of its yard. A 6 also earns another roll.
      • Tokens run clockwise around the board, then turn into their own coloured lane. An exact roll is needed to reach the centre.
      • Land on a square holding an opponent's token to send it back to its yard. Starting squares and starred squares are safe.
      • Capturing a token or reaching the centre earns a bonus roll. Three sixes in a row lose the turn.
      • Finish with all four tokens to take a place. Play continues until every place is decided.
